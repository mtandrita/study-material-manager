Help & Guide

📊 Dashboard

Your central hub for seeing all your important metrics at a glance. View your total hours studied, number of subjects, upcoming deadlines, and weekly goals.

⏱️ Pomodoro Timer

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method using a timer to break work into focused intervals and short breaks. Each cycle consists of:

  • 25 minutes of focused work (focus time)
  • 5 minutes of break time

Click "Start" to begin your focus session. The timer tracks your study time and adds it to your total hours studied on the dashboard.

📚 Subjects

Track all your courses for the semester. For each subject, you can store:

  • Subject name
  • Professor name
  • Class schedule
  • Current grade

📅 Important Dates

Keep track of all your deadlines and important events. You can categorize them as Exams, Assignments, Projects, Quizzes, Presentations, or Other. Dates are automatically sorted to show upcoming deadlines first.

🎯 Weekly Goals

Set weekly goals to keep yourself motivated and organized. You can prioritize each goal as High, Medium, or Low priority.

💡 Tips for Success

  • Use the Pomodoro Timer: Regular 25-minute focused sessions are more effective than long study periods.
  • Set Realistic Goals: Break down large tasks into smaller, manageable weekly goals.
  • Track Important Dates: Add deadlines as soon as you know about them to avoid surprises.
  • Update Your Grades: Keep your subject grades updated to monitor your progress.
  • Review Weekly: Check your dashboard regularly to stay on top of your commitments.
